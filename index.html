She doesn't search for art therapy, Imago or somatic work. She searches "how do I fix my marriage" or "why am I so tired all the time." So your doorway names what she's living right now, in her words. The art, the body and the relational work are what she finds once she's inside, and that's what makes you different.

Doorway one: couples who keep having the same fight
"I help couples who love each other but keep having the same fight learn to really hear each other again, and become partners who feel close instead of like two people running a household."

Who she is: a woman who loves her partner, keeps having the same fight, and isn't ready to give up. She's usually the one searching, so she's the one you speak to, but she's looking for help for the two of them.

Speaking to her directly: I help women who are tired of being the only one trying to fix their marriage bring their partner back into the conversation and feel like a team again.

Short version: I help couples stop having the same fight and start feeling close again.

From / to: I help couples go from the same argument on repeat to actually hearing each other and choosing each other again.

The between-sessions reset

Straight from your blueprint, for your open Solar Plexus and open Root. Use it between clients, and once at the end of your client day, so you don't carry people home with you.

  1. Step outside for five to ten minutes. Let your eyes rest on something living, a tree, the sky, the creek.
  2. Ask quietly: "Is what I'm feeling mine?" If it isn't, picture setting it down.
  3. At the end of the day, wash your hands slowly and on purpose, as the close of your client time.
  4. Before bed, a few minutes of Reiki on yourself. You give it to everyone else. You get to receive it too.
